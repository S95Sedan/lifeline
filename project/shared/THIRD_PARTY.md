# Third-party components

The game uses **SDL 3.4.12** for windowing, input, controllers, and audio.
SDL is linked statically and distributed under the zlib license.

- [SDL upstream](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL)
- [SDL license](../../docs/third-party/SDL-LICENSE.txt)
- [All third-party components](../../README.md#credits)

The SDL source download and SHA-256 are pinned in `cmake/Dependencies.cmake`.
